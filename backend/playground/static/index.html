Privacy

  
    
    
  • Faces are blurred in anything published, this page included.
    • 
    
  • Frames are not stored. Watch mode scores frames in memory and logs only the
      scores; the clips here were saved on purpose.
    • 
    
  • Generated images keep Muse Image's provenance: they are shown exactly as Muse
      Image made them. Images made for you go to your browser; the server keeps them only
      in its call cache, so the same request is not paid for twice.
    • 
    
  • Skills cover objects, states, text, displays, signs and vehicles. They never
      watch for specific people, bodies or health, and the glasses' capture light is
      never hidden.
    • 
    
  • This page loads nothing from other sites. A cookie with a random id, and your
      address, count your live spend; the address is stored only as a salted hash.
