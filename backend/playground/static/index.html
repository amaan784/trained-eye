    Privacy
    
  
    
    
    While watching, frames go from the glasses to your phone, then to your Mac and the
      model that scores them; before an alert is spoken, Muse Spark looks at that one frame
      to confirm it. Only the scores are written down. Frames are not stored unless you save
      a clip on purpose, and the clips here were saved that way.
    
    
    Faces are blurred in everything published, this page included.
    
    
    Images made for you go to your browser. The server keeps them only in its call cache,
      so the same request is not paid for twice, and they keep Muse Image's provenance,
      exactly as made.
    
    
    Skills watch objects, states, text, displays, signs and vehicles. They never watch
      for a particular person, for bodies or for health, and the glasses' capture light is
      never hidden.
    
    
    This page loads nothing from other sites. A cookie with a random id, and your address
      stored only as a salted hash, count what you spend live.
