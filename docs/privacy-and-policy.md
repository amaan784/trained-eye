# Privacy and policy

- No skill may identify or track a specific person. Skills cover objects, states, text,
  displays, signs and vehicles. The local policy check and Muse Wonder refuse the rest,
  and a refusal is explained in one sentence.
- No skills about bodies, health, medication or other sensitive traits; no covert
  watching; no sensitive places; no sports coaching; nothing about politics or war.
- Watching never hides or suppresses the glasses' capture LED, and watch mode is always
  visible in the app.
- While watching, frames are not stored unless the user saves a clip; watch mode keeps
  times, scores and decisions only. Teaching is different: the teaching frames and the
  request audio are kept on the Mac (data/teach/ and data/skills/, gitignored), since the
  skill is built from them.
- Muse Image outputs keep their watermark and provenance metadata. Generated images are
  never committed or published; only the prompt manifest and the script that regenerates
  them are. The one exception is live teaching in the playground, which shows each visitor
  the images made for their own request, in their browser only.
- Faces are blurred in anything published (`te blur`).
- The Meta Wearables Device Access Toolkit's analytics and crash reporting are turned off
  in the app.
